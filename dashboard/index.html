Training

Distillation from an expectimax teacher with a strictly proper loss: cross-entropy for the move, so the softmax is pushed toward calibrated probabilities. Held-out: every 50th teacher game, never trained on, for accuracy, ECE, Brier and NLL.

No training steps logged yet.

Machine

Teacher self-play on the pod's CPUs

How it is wired

Everything runs on one RunPod A100 pod. The CPU cores play teacher games while the GPU trains and evaluates.
